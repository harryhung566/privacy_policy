# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how idealAI ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the idealAI mobile and web apps ("the App"). idealAI is based in the United States and serves users worldwide.

## 1. Information We Collect
**Information you provide:**
- **Account information:** your name, email address, and phone number.
- **Content you post:** posts, comments, and other content you create in the App.

**Information collected automatically:**
- **Technical data:** device type, operating system, app version, IP address, and basic usage logs needed to keep the App secure and working.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
- Create and manage your account, and verify your identity.
- Provide, operate, and improve the App and its features, including AI-powered features.
- Communicate with you about your account, security, and important updates.
- Prevent abuse, fraud, and violations of our [Terms & Conditions](terms).
- Comply with legal obligations.

## 3. AI Features
Some features use third-party AI providers (such as OpenAI or Anthropic) to process your requests. When you use these features, the relevant content is sent to these providers only to generate a response. [We do not permit these providers to use your data to train their models.]

## 4. How We Share Your Information
We share information only:
- **With service providers** who help us run the App (such as AI providers and cloud hosting), under contracts that require them to protect your data.
- **With other users**, for content you choose to make visible in the App.
- **When required by law**, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and the App.
- **In a business transfer**, such as a merger or acquisition, with notice to you.

## 5. Legal Basis (EU/EEA and UK users)
We process your data based on: performing our contract with you (providing the App), our legitimate interests (security and improvement), your consent (where required), and compliance with legal obligations.

## 6. International Transfers
Your data may be processed in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 7. Data Retention
We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data within [30] days, unless we must keep it longer for legal reasons.

## 8. Your Rights
Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR and California law), you may have the right to:
- Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
- Export your data in a portable format.
- Object to or restrict certain processing.
- Withdraw consent at any time.
- Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

You can delete your account [in the App's Settings] or exercise any of these rights by emailing support@theideal.ai. We will respond within 30 days.

## 9. Security
We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, such as encryption in transit, to protect your information. No system is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data.

## 10. Children's Privacy
The App is not intended for children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU/EEA). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 11. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above and notify you in the App of significant changes.

## 12. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@theideal.ai.
